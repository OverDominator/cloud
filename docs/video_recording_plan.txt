Demonstration recording plan

Purpose: show what the robot is asked to do, the information it receives, the
navigation decisions, and actual rescue completion in one identifiable run.

Layout: Unity observer overview on the left, robot camera and RViz on the right.
Observer overview must be labelled as an external observer, not robot knowledge.
Robot image should come from the actual published image topic where practical;
the current Main Camera preview is not automatically identical to that topic.
RViz should show the robot, point cloud, current goal and route, not every debug
marker. All three views must refer to the same live run.

Sequence: explain scene and four-victim task; show exploration and guidance;
show approach and at least one rescue confirmation; finish with COMPLETE and
four rescued victims. Retain the uncut recording and matching logs. Mark any
speed-up or editing. Manual control, if demonstrated, belongs in a separate clip.

Observer tool currently exists only in the verification copy under
Fire Rescue / Video / Observer overview. Its 10 fps preview is not a recorder.
Do not claim a 30 fps source merely because an encoder outputs 30 fps.

Preflight: confirm rendering, model-ready status, separate logs, sufficient disk,
readable status labels, and recording performance. Avoid changing the robot's
sensor rate/resolution or physics for visual appearance. Added observer rendering
can still affect performance; check before presenting recorded-run timings.
